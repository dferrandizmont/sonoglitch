# Code of conduct

We want Sonoglitch to be a welcoming place to learn, report problems and contribute. Treat people with respect, including when you disagree. Explain technical concerns clearly, accept constructive feedback and make room for different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks and publishing someone's private information without consent are not welcome. Do not use issues, reviews or other project spaces to intimidate people or pursue unwanted contact.

These expectations apply in project spaces and when representing the project elsewhere. Maintainers should apply them fairly and can edit or remove harmful content, pause a discussion, restrict participation or exclude someone for serious or repeated misconduct. When practical, they will explain the decision and allow clarification.

To raise a concern, write privately to dev@mainshai.com or contact a repository maintainer through a contact method they explicitly provide on GitHub. If neither is available, ask only for a private contact channel without identifying affected people or describing sensitive events publicly. Share only the information needed to understand the concern. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and avoid involving anyone who has a conflict of interest; confidentiality cannot be guaranteed.

This policy uses our own wording and is inspired by the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
